Frequently Asked Questions About SEO

Common questions about search engine optimization.

What is SEO?

SEO, or search engine optimization, is the process of improving a website so search engines can better understand its content and users can find relevant pages through organic search.

What are SEO services?

SEO services can include technical SEO, keyword research, content optimization, internal linking, website structure improvements and conversion-focused optimization.

How long does SEO take to show results?

SEO timelines vary depending on competition, website quality, content, technical factors and search engine changes. Some improvements may appear within a few months, while significant results can take six months to a year or longer.

Why is technical SEO important?

Technical SEO helps search engines crawl, understand and index a website. It also supports website structure, mobile usability and page performance.
